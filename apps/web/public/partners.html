Why this exists

Collections are your most expensive, least profitable hours

Every home collection you run yourself costs a driver's time, a van, fuel, and insurance — £25 or more per pickup once the day is honestly costed. Bookings arrive scattered across the week and across the map, so the van runs half-empty or the customer waits.

Faira pools collections from multiple shipping companies into one efficient driver round. Density we can build together is density none of us can build alone — that's what makes each pickup cheaper than running it yourself.

How it works

From booking link to your warehouse door

  1. 1

    Set up in one afternoon you

    Register your company, add your warehouse address and opening hours, and get your own branded booking link to share on WhatsApp, Facebook, or your website.

  2. 2

    Customers book — no account, no app faira

    They enter their address, what they're sending (drums, suitcases, fridges, pallets…) and how many parcels. They get a reference, a live tracking link, and a confirmation text.

  3. 3

    Watch demand build, then declare your collection week you

    Your dashboard shows how many bookings and parcels are waiting. When you have the volume and you're ready to receive — typically in the run-up to your monthly shipment — you set your collection week. Every waiting customer is told their week is set, automatically.

  4. 4

    We route, collect, and prove it faira

    Faira plans the driver rounds inside your week. Each customer is texted their exact day. At the doorstep: photo proof, and a printed label on every box — customer name, your company, destination, "parcel 2 of 3".

  5. 5

    Parcels arrive at your warehouse faira

    Delivered labelled and accounted for, ready for you to weigh, pack, and ship exactly as you do today.

  6. 6

    You confirm handover — that's the only moment you're charged you

    One click per booking confirms receipt. Your billing statement groups every charge by collection week, so one shipment cycle = one clean statement.
